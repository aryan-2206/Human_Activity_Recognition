Static activities (SITTING, STANDING, LAYING) typically show lower short-term motion variability, while dynamic activities (WALKING, WALKING_UPSTAIRS, WALKING_DOWNSTAIRS) show stronger periodic changes. Posture-related differences between sitting and standing can be subtle, and body orientation affects the axes. Plots use train-fitted standardized channels, so magnitudes are in standardized units, not m/s². The UCI HAR dataset does not include running.
